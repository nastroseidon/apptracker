Import from iPhone Storage

  1. On your iPhone open Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
  2. Wait for the app list to finish loading, then screenshot it. Scroll and screenshot again until you have the whole list.
  3. Open a screenshot in Photos, tap the Live Text button (bottom-right), Select All, then Copy.
  4. Paste below. Repeat for each screenshot — importing again updates apps you already have instead of duplicating them.
